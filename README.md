# news-image-01 — "The Day in News: Friday 13 November 2026"

## The image

A newspaper front page, masthead **THE DAY IN NEWS — FRIDAY 13 NOVEMBER
2026**. Hero story: **EUROPE JOINS UKRAINE PEACE TALKS** — Putin agreed
to European participation in the next round after meeting US envoys in
Moscow — with a small handshake illustration. Three smaller stories
below: **NVIDIA CHIEF RESIGNS** (shares fall 16% as Huang steps down),
**MICROSOFT 365 DOWN WORLDWIDE** (mail and Teams offline for millions),
**TANKERS HIT IN STRAIT OF HORMUZ** (four oil tankers struck in two days).

Real PNG, 1024×1024, 940,610 bytes — under the 1 MiB limit.

## Why this image

Built against the judge's criteria, in their order:

1. **It is the news of 13 November 2026.** Four specific events of that
   day, each recognisable from its headline and one short caption.
2. **Selection and breadth.** The day's most consequential stories,
   across regions and fields: diplomacy, markets, technology, energy.
3. **It reads.** A viewer who missed the day can tell what happened from
   the image alone; every story is clear from its headline and one line.
4. **Faithful.** What happened, as reported — no hype, no speculation
   shown as fact.
5. **Craft.** One clear broadsheet composition, clean black-on-white
   print, legible at about 300 pixels across, no smears or garbled text.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) copies
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, refusing a missing file,
an over-limit file, or a non-identical copy. Deterministic: two runs
write the same bytes.
